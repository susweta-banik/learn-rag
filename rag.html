What is happening under the hood?

Think of RAG as two systems working together: a retrieval system that finds knowledge, and a generation system that uses that knowledge to answer the question.

1 · Indexing Documents are loaded, split into chunks, converted into embeddings, and stored in a vector index.
2 · Query The user's question is converted into a representation that can be compared with the indexed chunks.
3 · Retrieval The system ranks chunks by relevance and selects the top results.
4 · Generation Retrieved context is added to the prompt and supplied to an LLM to produce the final response.
